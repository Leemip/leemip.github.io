# Creased Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

Creased does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **Fold counts** (lifetime, today and session) are stored only on your device,
  in storage shared between the app and its widget. They are never sent to us.
- **Game Center:** if you're signed in to Game Center, your lifetime and daily
  fold counts are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles
  that data under its own privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in
  Settings at any time.
- **No tracking, analytics, advertising or accounts.** Creased contains no
  third-party SDKs.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Questions: contact the developer through the support link on the App Store page.
